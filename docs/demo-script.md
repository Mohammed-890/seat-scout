# Two-minute demo script

**0:00 – What it is (15 s).** "Seat Scout is a personal prototype exploring AI-assisted seat selection for airline customer service. The layout, prices and availability are illustrative; nothing is booked."

**0:15 – Find my seat (30 s).** Press **I want to sleep.** The adviser answers with one seat: "You asked for a window if possible and a quieter zone. This seat matches both. The trade-off is passing two seats to reach the aisle." Press **View seat**, then **Window**.

**0:45 – The seat (30 s).** The seat pass shows the price, two advantages and one trade-off. Open **Seat details**: pitch is row spacing; the space at knee height is a modelled estimate. Open **Explore this seat**: lower the shade, open the tray, then **Recline preview** to see the effect on the seat behind.

**1:15 – Change of plan (25 s).** Type "Two adults and a 5-year-old, £45 total, together", then "Actually, £25 maximum." Only the budget changes, and the earlier suggestion is marked outdated. Try "1 adult, window, total £2": no seat matches, and the adviser lists what would work and asks before relaxing anything.

**1:40 – Compare and select (20 s).** **Compare** two seats: price difference, access and recline. **Select (demo)** re-checks availability and confirms nothing was booked; **Change** clears it.

**2:00 – Under the hood (optional).** **About this project**: which parts use AI and which use code, the execution trace, the deterministic evaluation and the recovery demos.
