    How these are measured

    Seat pitch: the distance between the same point on this seat and the seat in front (dataset value). Modelled space at knee height: measured in the 3D model, 60 cm above the floor, from the front of your seat back to the back of the seat ahead. Estimated body clearance: that space minus a buttock-to-knee length taken as 33% of the height you enter, an adult average. It's an estimate: height alone doesn't predict comfort, because thigh length, posture and a reclined seat ahead all change it.
