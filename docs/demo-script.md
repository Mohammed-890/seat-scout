# Two-minute demo script

**0:00 – What it is (15 s).** "Seat Scout is an independent concept demo of AI-assisted seat selection. The layout, prices and availability are illustrative; nothing is booked."

**0:15 – Choose a seat (30 s).** Click **14A** in the aircraft or on the seat map. The panel shows price, seat pitch, window position and aisle access: "You would pass two seats to reach the aisle." Press **From seat**, then **Window**, then **Legroom** (the modelled space at knee height).

**0:45 – Ask the assistant (35 s).** Paste:
> We're two adults travelling with a five-year-old. Our total seat budget is £45. We want to sit together, and I'd prefer a window away from the wing.

One best recommendation appears with its total, per-seat prices and a short reason. Open **other options** and **Why this recommendation** to show the detail is there but out of the way. Press **View seat**.

**1:20 – Change of plan (25 s).** "Actually, £25 maximum." Only the budget changes, and the earlier card is marked outdated with its Select button disabled. **Select (demo)** on the new card re-checks availability and confirms nothing was booked.

**1:45 – Under the hood (15 s).** **About this project**: the execution trace (tool calls, exclusions, validation), the deterministic evaluation (47 cases, with numerators and denominators) and the recovery demos (seat taken, impossible budget, tool timeout). Live-model evaluation: not run, and the app says so.
